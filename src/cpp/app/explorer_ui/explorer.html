Bitcoin UTXO Explorer

Every unspent coin on the Bitcoin blockchain, as one pixel, from the genesis block to today. Play it as a video, pause anywhere, click any pixel to see exactly which coins were there.

Reading the picture

Left → rightTime, by block height. The newest blocks are drawn at the cursor on the right; older history is progressively squeezed to the left so 17 years fit on one screen. Bottom → topAmount of the coin, log scale from 1 sat to 100 k BTC. The 1–100 sat rows and the 10 k–100 k BTC band are compressed. ColourHow many coins share the pixel (log scale). Coins above 5 BTC count as amount ÷ 5, so one 100 BTC coin weighs like twenty ordinary ones. BlackNothing unspent there any more. Spent coins disappear.

White flashes mark coins created or spent in the current block; the bigger the star, the more BTC moved. Orange bars at the cursor show where new coins are being created right now.

Using it

Click a pixelOpens the details drawer: which coins were there at that block, their amounts, when they were created, and whether they are still unspent today. HoverThe strip at the bottom shows the block range, dates and amount range under the cursor. Aim preciselyPause, then use the magnifier (click inside it to pick), or nudge with ← → ↑ ↓ (Shift = 10 px). Move in timeScrub the bar, type a block number, pick a date, or step with [ ] and the < > buttons. Sharecopy link gives a URL that reopens this exact block and pixel.
