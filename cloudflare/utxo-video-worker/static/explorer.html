UTXO Timelapse

Bitcoin’s unspent outputs, mapped by their creation block and amount. Many outputs can share one pixel. The published timeline runs from genesis to the data cutoff shown below. Play it as a video, pause anywhere, click any pixel to inspect the outputs represented there.

Reading the picture

Left → rightTime, by block height. The newest blocks are drawn at the cursor on the right; older history is progressively squeezed to the left so 17 years fit on one screen. Bottom → topAmount of the coin, log scale from 1 sat to 100 k BTC. The 1–100 sat rows and the 10 k–100 k BTC band are compressed. ColourAccumulated output density (log scale). Coins above 5 BTC count as amount ÷ 5, so one 100 BTC coin weighs like twenty ordinary ones. BlackNothing unspent there any more. Spent coins disappear.

White flashes mark coins created or spent in the current block; the bigger the star, the more BTC moved. Orange bars at the cursor show where new coins are being created right now.

At 1440p, magnifier colours are resampled. Pixel coordinates and coin lookups use the 4K master grid.

Using it

Click a pixelOpens the details drawer: which coins were there at that block, their amounts, when they were created, and whether they remained unspent at the published data cutoff. HoverThe strip at the bottom shows the block range, dates and amount range under the cursor. Aim preciselyPause, then use the magnifier (click inside it to pick), or nudge with ← → ↑ ↓ (Shift = 10 px). Move in timeScrub the bar, type a block number, pick a date, or step with [ ] or use the controls to step by 1 or 20 blocks. Playback speedUse Speed to slow the film down to 0.1× or speed it up to 4×. One frame still represents one block. ShareShare this view lets you share the block with autoplay on or off, or a selected pixel paused with its details open. Copy the link or share through X.com, email or your device.
